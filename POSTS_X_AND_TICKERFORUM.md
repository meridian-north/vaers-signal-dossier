## TickerForum (long-form)

**Title:** We rebuilt VAERS clean in a weekend — here's the cross-national signal, and the denominator they won't give us

Two people and a weekend, with AI assistance, produced something the public
vaccine-safety apparatus has not: an open, searchable, reproducible
rebuild of VAERS, plus a signal analysis across five independent national
reporting systems. Everything below is computed from the raw public data and is
reproducible — same input, same method, same numbers.

**The signal is real and it isn't American.** The myocarditis pattern — young
male, dose-related — converges across VAERS (US), MHRA (UK), TGA (AU), JADER (JP),
and CVAR (CA). A control event (anaphylaxis) correctly comes out the opposite way
in all five, so this isn't a tool that flags everything. A pattern that repeats
across five systems with no shared plumbing is not a reporting artifact.

**It's a signal — not a proven link.** That distinction is the whole point.
Disproportionality is the alarm; confirming a link needs controlled studies, which
a reporting system can't do alone. We label it that way on every chart, and we
flag the death signal as the weakest and most confounded rather than inflating it.

**The thing they all lack: a denominator.** You can't turn report counts into a
rate without knowing the doses. That denominator exists — dose data, active
cohorts, registries — and it's publicly funded. It just isn't joined to the signal
in real time; it gets reconstructed years later. The reasonable demand is simple:
release it, in real time, so anyone can compute the rate.

Look at the charts and check the math yourself:
**https://meridian-north.github.io/vaers-signal-dossier/**
